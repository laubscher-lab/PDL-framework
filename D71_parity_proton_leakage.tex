\begin{abstract}
\noindent We report a mechanism-level result inside the coherence-network corpus (D-series): an odd-parity sea of $11017 = 23 \times 479$ relations on the proton surface forces exactly one conserved, mobile matching defect (a \emph{monomer}), whose circulation, localised by an Euler condition at two degree-25 vertices on the bow of the spin-carrying valence core, yields an effective boundary count $B_{\mathrm{eff}} = 528 + \tfrac{1}{2\sqrt{2}} + \tfrac{1}{56}$. This single mechanism, with all coefficients derived in closed form from the architecture integers, is simultaneously compatible with three independent observational targets: the proton-skin amplitude $\Dr = 0.04795969 \pm 6.98\times 10^{-8}$ controlling the $\alpha \leftrightarrow G$ bridge (residual $0.64\sigma$), the closed fraction $f = 0.157126 \pm 9\times 10^{-4}$ (candidate for $\Omega_b/\Omega_m$; residual $0.10\sigma$), and the valence spin share $\Delta\Sigma = 0.30 \pm 0.05$ (EMC/COMPASS; residual $0.17\sigma$). We distinguish carefully between theorem-level, derived-within-declared-family, and compatibility-level statements, and we show that this result does \emph{not} yet lift the circularity of $G$: a continuous derivation C1--C4 $\to B \to \Dr \to \varepsilon_G \to G_{\mathrm{PDL}}$ remains to be written. A negative dossier (campaigns v3--v9) that eliminates six alternative branches is included as an integral part of the evidence.
\end{abstract}

\section{Euler localisation of the defect (v13)}
\textbf{(D)} An alternating defect trail on the wake boundary is Eulerian iff the number of odd-degree vertices of the front is $0$ or $2$. The diagonal interface of a core is $12$ (even); hence the degree excess must arrive \emph{in pairs}: two degree-25 vertices, not one of degree 26 (which would break alternation). Scenario exclusion fixes both loci \emph{on the bow of the reference (spin-carrying) core}. Per locus, the diagonal-stub correction is

\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\times\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{1}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{8}, \qquad 2\ \text{loci} \Rightarrow \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}},

\section{Limitations and falsifiers}\label{sec:limits}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em]
\item The fractional T1 hit is at $\sim 1.7\sigma$ (Poisson $\lambda = 0.064$, $P(\geq 1) = 6.2\%$): a serious indication, not a discovery.
\item The 7 T2 hits are correlated (single counting cluster); the nominal $9\times10^{-8}$ overstates significance. Selection was by structural criterion, not by fit.
\item T3's EMC window ($\pm 0.05$) is wide: T3 confirms without strongly discriminating.
\item The matching-model reduction (sea as dimer/monomer problem under the $2^{1-k}$ law) is itself level (D), not (T); the graph spectral dimension $\approx 2$ (the \emph{surface} claim) remains to be established, not assumed.
\item The ``external'' agent maintaining total spin $1/2$ (the closure wave) must be identified: condition at measurement, or global 930-closure as integral constraint.
\item \textbf{G remains circular.} What is achieved is a fully specified mechanism whose every coefficient is derived in closed form; what is missing is the continuous demonstration C1--C4 $\to B \to \Dr \to \varepsilon_G \to G_{\mathrm{PDL}}$ that never consults the measured $\alpha$.
\end{enumerate}
\noindent\textbf{Engraved falsifier (pre-registered):} any admissible mechanism must reproduce \emph{simultaneously} $\Dr = 0.04795969 \pm 6.98\times10^{-8}$ and $f = 0.157126 \pm 9\times10^{-4}$ from a single law; a law predicting only one of the two is dead.
