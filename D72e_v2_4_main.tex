\begin{abstract}\noindent
This edition of D72e documents the 38-gate campaign carried out on the frozen PDL chain (passes v25--v70) and supersedes v2.3, whose text it keeps in substance while restoring the gate ledger of v2--v2.2, a fully computational reproduction package, and an explicitly defined selector filter. The frozen prediction $G_{\mathrm{pred}} = 6.6743264\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{m^3\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$ stands at $+3.96\ppm = +0.18\sigma$ against the CODATA 2022 recommended value and has not been re-fitted since the freeze at pass v53; the inherited $\sim 20\%$ probability of a fortuitous agreement is retained as a central reserve, and $x = 7/12$ is carried as a reading retained between v50 and v52 in view of the $G$-derived target $\delta \approx 3.0430\%$. The $\delta$ dressing series of gate~32 is a post-hoc consistency test: its fourth-order coefficient saturates at $c_4 = 499.73$, and any $c_4$ between 69 and 931 keeps $\varepsilon$ within its own uncertainty. The pre-registered falsifier reduces to a single leg: the radial residual $d_r$ at $+41.547\ppm = 28.55\sigma$, while the tangential leg $f$ was never in tension ($-33.010\ppm$ against $u(f) = 5.73\times10^{3}\ppm$, propagated from Planck via D70/D71). The single tension is closed sub-ppm by the signed curvature of the 918-site boundary, $c = (2\sin(\pi/918))^2 = 46.846018\ppm$, with $k_d = 8/9$: one coefficient for one datum, graded form-hit. Under the axioms A1--A4, formulated after gate~37 and here given a computable definition of description length, an exhaustive filter over 1826 radial candidates leaves six admissible pairs, and twelve radial coefficients fit within $u(d_r)$; no uniqueness is claimed. The rehabilitation of $77/10240$ and the statistical promotion of gate~29 remain retracted ($p_{\mathrm{post}} = 0.9902$ and $0.3370$). Every number quoted is printed by one of fourteen scripts that compute it from the frozen chain and exit with status~0 only if the stated verdict holds.
\end{abstract}

\section{Gate 29 and the $77/10240$ window}
The candidate $77/10240$ lies $+16.334\ppm$ from the chain value of $\varepsilon$. Under the canonical convention, with the cascade window and a census of $N = 1100$, its posterior probability is $p_{\mathrm{post}} = 0.9902$: it does not rise above the campaign background. The dressing $k = 1-(v/c)^2/3$, with $v = \alpha c$, brings the candidate to $-1.417\ppm$, that is $-1.16\,u(\varepsilon)$, with $p_{\mathrm{post}} = 0.3370$ at $N = 1128$ (the withdrawn figure was $0.0032$). The residual is kept on record as a measured value; gate~29 is a form-hit.

\section{The dressing series of the frozen $\delta$}\label{sec:delta}
Gate~32 dresses the frozen chain value as
\begin{equation}
\varepsilon = \frac{77}{10240}\Big[1 - \tfrac13\Big(\frac{v}{c}\Big)^2 + c_4\Big(\frac{v}{c}\Big)^4\Big], \qquad v = \alpha c .
\end{equation}
Saturating the residual of the frozen value fixes $c_4 = 499.73$. The declared members $918000/\mu = 499.958$, $500$, $16524/33$ and $11017/22$ all leave residuals below $0.003\ppm$. Because $(v/c)^4 = 2.8\times10^{-9}$, any $c_4$ between 69 and 931 keeps $\varepsilon$ within $u(\varepsilon) = 1.22\ppm$: the fourth-order coefficient is not constrained by the data, and the reading $918/1.836 = 500$ rests on the identity $1.836 = 918/500$ and a truncated mass ratio. The series therefore tests the arithmetic coherence of the frozen value and derives nothing.

\section{The curvature mechanism and the selector}
\subsection{Mechanism (form-hit)}
Treating the boundary as a regular polygon of 918 sites, the discrete signed curvature is $c = (2\sin(\pi/918))^2 = 46.846018\ppm$, differing from the continuum form $(2\pi/918)^2$ by $3.9\times10^{-6}$ in relative value. The mechanism is applied as $d_r \to d_r(1-k_d c)$ and $f \to f(1+k_f c)$; the opposite signs are part of its definition. With $k_d = 8/9$ and $k_f = 1/\sqrt2$ \citep{berry1984} the residuals are $-0.0957\ppm$ and $+0.1142\ppm$ (with the continuum form, $-0.0958\ppm$ and $+0.1143\ppm$).

\subsection{The A1--A4 filter, defined and computed}
The axioms were formulated after gate~37 had revealed the refuter cloud; the filter below is therefore post hoc and conditional. For a rational $p/q$ in lowest terms: A1 requires every prime factor of $p$ and $q$ to lie in $\{2,3,7,11,17\}$; A2 requires $L(p/q) = \Omega(p)+\Omega(q) \le 6$, where $\Omega$ counts prime factors with multiplicity (v2.3 left $L$ undefined; this definition is introduced here); A3 fixes the signed-curvature mechanism and $0<k_d<1$; A4 admits for $k_f$ the surd $1/\sqrt2$ or a convergent of it satisfying A1--A2. The joint window is the one declared at gate~34, $|r|\le0.5\ppm$ on both legs.

The radial alphabet contains 1826 candidates. The convergents of $1/\sqrt2$ are $1$, $2/3$, $5/7$, $12/17$, $29/41$, $70/99$, and so on; A1--A2 retain $1$, $2/3$ and $12/17$. Six pairs pass: $k_d \in \{68/77,\ 8/9,\ 121/136\}$ crossed with $k_f \in \{1/\sqrt2,\ 12/17\}$, with radial residuals $+0.175$, $-0.096$ and $-0.134\ppm$ and tangential residuals $+0.114$ and $+0.057\ppm$. The pair $(8/9, 12/17)$ is tighter than the champion on $f$. At the actual precision of the radial target, twelve radial coefficients of the alphabet fall within $u(d_r)$, from $6/7$ to $343/374$. The selector is admissible, not unique. A route to a genuine uniqueness statement would be a first-principles proof that the Berry double cover forces $1/\sqrt2$ exactly; it is recorded as an open debt.

\subsection{Two retired instruments}
\textbf{D1.} The ratio $k_d/k_f$ is compared with the ratio $k_d^{\mathrm{req}}/k_f^{\mathrm{req}} = 1.2585$ of the coefficients that close each leg exactly. Its distance from that target is a fixed function of the two residuals already reported, and the script reconstructs it from them to $10^{-9}$: D1 contains no independent information. \textbf{The link quantum.} $q_{\mathrm{link}} = k_d\,[c(918)-c(919)] = 0.0906\ppm$, that is $0.062\,u(d_r)$. Statements made at this scale are internal coherence observations and carry no empirical weight.
